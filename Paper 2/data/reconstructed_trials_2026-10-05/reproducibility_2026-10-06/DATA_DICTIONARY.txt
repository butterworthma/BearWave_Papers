Reception input
record_id: stable upstream reconstruction identifier; unique within the inventory.
window_id: retrospective W1–W7 assignment, independently checked by date/frequency.
recorded_timestamp: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS; UTC for Borneo receiver records.
dial_frequency_MHz: internal receiver-tuning context, MHz; not inferred from filename.
SNR_dB: JS8Call decoder SNR estimate among successful decodes, dB.
DT_seconds: decoder timing offset, seconds; not message latency.
audio_offset_Hz: decoded audio offset, Hz.
category: CQ = controlled test message; heartbeat; ID_only = identifier-only record.
in_previous_spreadsheets: True/False; membership of the older selected reception set.
source_locations: original relative log filenames and line numbers; multiple copies separated by semicolons.
frequency_header_locations: source lines providing internal frequency context.

Window input
UTC_date_inclusive / UTC_date_exclusive: descriptive partition boundaries, not operating times.
frequency_MHz: exact band value required for the window rule.

Map inputs
filename: original map image filename, not a local computer path.
timestamp_UTC: filename-derived map time.
sha256: digest of the full original PNG; originals are not bundled.
dx,dy: pixel displacement from (453,220); integers -3..3.
red,green,blue: original 8-bit RGB channels, unmodified.
class_MHz: integer legend colour class; approximate foF2 scale in MHz.
r1,r2,r3 in derived tables: radius 1,2,3 pixels, i.e. 3x3,5x5,7x7 patches.
*_mode: unique modal matched colour class; blank when tied/unresolved.
*_min,*_max: extreme matching palette classes in the patch, not measurement error bounds.
*_pixels: count of exact palette-colour matches in the patch.
map_minus_receive_seconds: map timestamp minus reception timestamp; within +/-450 s.
*_comparison_1MHz_buffer: below, near, above, unresolved relative to modal class +/-1 MHz.

Station inputs
April_day: continuous UTC day-of-month in April 2023, including fractional day.
foF2_MHz: value from the author's 2023 series, unchanged.

Power-chart inputs
chart: chart1.xml, chart3.xml or chart4.xml in the supplied trial-report DOCX.
point_index: zero-based point index in that chart's cached series.
recorded_timestamp: UTC time reconstructed from plotted clock and documented date.
nominal_power_W: documentary chart label, not a per-reception measurement.

Pi input/results
recorded_time: logger time of day; time zone unspecified.
temperature_C: measured processor temperature, degrees Celsius.
CPU_MHz/core_MHz: reported clock frequencies, MHz.
health_bits: original binary status string; leading zeros preserved.
Vcore_V: reported core voltage, volts.
*_current/*_history: binary status flags, not independent event counts.

Missing values and units
Empty numeric cells mean unavailable/not applicable; they are never replaced by zero.
Missing maps and empty SNR time bins are not interpolated.
Quartiles use type-7 interpolation; medians and ranges describe observations, not uncertainty in a population mean.
