# Field documentation

This collection connects the April 2023 Borneo field trial to the current numerical analysis.

- [Trial plan: original PDF](Trial_Plan_original.pdf) — unchanged planning document, 27 PDF pages (printed pages 0–26).
- [Trial plan and implementation](trial_plan_and_implementation.md) — original objectives, actual implementation and links to the analysed outcomes.
- [Equipment and deployment](equipment_and_deployment.md) — hardware, antennas, software, timing, power measurement and site context.
- [Dated chronology](trial_chronology.csv) — dated observations and later clarifications, with their sources and time precision.
- [Photograph gallery](photos/README.md) — selected equipment, installation, canopy and access images from the author's field report.
- [Source inventory](source_inventory.json) — source filenames, SHA-256 hashes and publication scope.

The trial plan describes intended work and equipment options. It is a historical document, not the final operating record, a preregistration of the later analysis, or a current technical/regulatory reference. The current interpretation is set out in the [analysis protocol](../data/reconstructed_trials_2026-10-05/reproducibility_2026-10-06/ANALYSIS_PROTOCOL_v1.3.txt).

The photo captions identify their documentary context; a date is assigned only where the report supplies it. No precise photograph capture time or per-reception site assignment is inferred. The selected embedded images are copied without pixel changes. Their source metadata contains no GPS location tags. Identifiable people and photographs with embedded remote-site coordinates are excluded from this selected gallery.

Original copyright and third-party credits remain with their respective holders. The author's supplied report is the source of the selected photos; no additional reuse licence is asserted here. The full unpublished diary, personal licensing/payment documents and travel paperwork are not included.
